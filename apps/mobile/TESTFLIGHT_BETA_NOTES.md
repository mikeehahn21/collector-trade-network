# Konnesor TestFlight Beta Notes

## Public Beta Summary

Konnesor helps vintage collectors document tradeable pieces, rank wishlist grails, compose structured trade proposals, and keep item-specific conversations in one place.

## Tester Instructions

1. Open the app and wait for the Konnesor logo intro.
2. Open `Open tester guide` from Home and follow the guided four-part test script.
3. Tap `Seed demo data` or `Load demo loop` if the account has no data yet.
4. Review Archive item proof, including photos, measurements, estimated value, and the 5 second clip slot.
5. Add one item photo from Camera and one from Library, then report if either path fails.
6. Open Comp Finder on an Archive item, add search clues, check eBay sold and one other source, then save a value range.
7. Review Wishlist ranking and grail priority.
8. Compose one trade proposal, review the trade safety checklist, then open the linked message thread.
9. Save one feedback entry on Home with what worked, what broke, and the screen where it happened.

## Known Beta Limits

- Demo/local fallback records are stored on the device for testing.
- The trade flow does not execute payments, shipping labels, or real escrow.
- Short item videos are capped around 5 seconds so scrolling stays fast.
- Comp Finder opens external marketplace searches; it does not yet import or verify third-party listing prices automatically.
- Some live API calls may fall back to local mode while the backend is being hardened.

## Feedback To Collect

- Did the black, white, and orange visual system feel premium?
- Could a seller understand what photos or proof they need to upload?
- Did Comp Finder help the tester find useful comps faster than searching manually?
- Which comp sources were most useful: eBay sold, Google, Grailed, Depop, Etsy, Mercari, or Poshmark?
- Did the trade proposal flow make the next step obvious?
- Did the trade safety checklist make the swap feel more trustworthy?
- Did empty states tell the tester exactly what to do next?
- What confused the tester before they could complete the loop?

# Product Source of Truth

The approved product foundation for implementation:

- Business + Product Foundation.
- MVP Product & User Experience Blueprint.
- Founder Decisions.
- Approved Contextual Messaging Model.

## V1 Product Objective

Help serious collectors complete trusted trades that improve their collections.

## Locked V1 Decisions

- Launch mode is invite-only, with architecture supporting waitlist,
  applications, admin approval, and public signup later.
- Initial category is vintage T-shirts.
- Initial trades are mostly shipped.
- Initial users are seller-collectors.
- Cash adjustments are supported, but cash-only buying/selling is not core.
- Communication must always belong to an item, trade, or system context.
- No unrestricted direct messaging in V1.
- V1 excludes showcase collections, following, activity feeds, and generic social
  feeds.

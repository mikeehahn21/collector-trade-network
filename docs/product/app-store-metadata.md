## Full Description

**Konnesor** is the premier invite-only platform for trading high-end vintage and luxury streetwear. Built for serious collectors, we've replaced the traditional cash marketplace with a pure, deterministic trading engine.

Discover grails, build your digital archive, and execute secure trades with verified counterparties.

**FEATURES**

- **Pure Trading Engine:** No buy-it-now prices, no cash offers. Match your collection against others using our deterministic Trade Graph Engine.
- **Verified Counterparties:** Every user goes through a rigorous onboarding process, including proof-of-life video verification and AI review, ensuring you only trade with real, trusted collectors.
- **Reputation System:** Build your Trust Score through successful trades and responsive communication. Unlock Elite Collector status for increased visibility.
- **Secure Execution:** Contextual messaging tied directly to specific items and trades. Coordinated shipping tracking ensures both parties are protected until delivery is confirmed.
- **Digital Archive:** Manage your inventory with high-resolution photo galleries and detailed metadata. Keep a private Wishlist to signal demand to the matching engine.

_Note: Konnesor is currently in an invite-only beta. You can download the app and join the waitlist, but an invite code is required to activate your account._
